Frequently asked questions

Do I apply through BEC?

No. Every Apply button takes you to the employer’s own application page.

Is it free?

Yes, for students, graduates and employers.

What does “Rolling applications” mean?

The employer has no fixed closing date and reviews applications as they arrive. Apply early, as these can close without notice.

I found a listing that’s closed or wrong.

Email us the listing title and we’ll check it within one working day.
