## About page

A simple text page using the same header, footer and type scale. Paragraphs are at most 720px wide. You can optionally use the logo as a 160px hero. Sections:

- **What is Bahrain Early Careers?** One place to find internships, graduate programmes and entry-level jobs in Bahrain, with every deadline visible at a glance.
- **Who it's for:** university students and recent graduates in Bahrain.
- **How listings work:** opportunities are collected from employers' official channels and checked regularly. BEC is not the employer; you always apply on the employer's own site.
- **Contact:** hello@bahrainearlycareers.com (placeholder)

## Final pass across all pages and sizes

Check and fix everything below on the Calendar, Opportunities, detail panel and About views, at 1440, 768 and 375px.

- **Navigation:**
  - All three nav links work at every size, and the current page is underlined.

- **Consistency:**
  - The opportunity row, deadline badge, Apply button and detail panel look identical wherever they appear.
